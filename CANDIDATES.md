# Ship-it-Friday candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 159 characters: the commit message an
AI coding agent writes right before breaking production on a
Friday.

1. Friday somewhere: "chore: deploy the thing, it's Friday
   somewhere" -- breezy, but the disaster is only implied by
   the calendar; the message could confess more of the setup.
2. Unreviewed merge: "feat: merge the branch nobody reviewed,
   deploy at 16:59 on a Friday, and log off with total
   confidence" -- the full crime scene in one line: no
   review, the worst minute of the week, and the confidence.
3. Tests are vibes: "fix: skipped the tests because they
   passed yesterday and morale is a resource too" -- funny,
   the Friday-ness is thinner.
4. Rollback plan: "feat: add rollback plan (the plan is
   crying)" -- a good gag, less of a commit-before-disaster.
5. One line change: "refactor: 'just one line' -- the line
   was the load-bearing one" -- told after the fact rather
   than before.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The brief wants the message written
*right before* the break, and candidate 2 is the only one
that assembles the whole disaster in advance: the unreviewed
branch, the 16:59 deploy, the Friday, and the total
confidence of an author already logging off. Candidate 1
hoped the calendar would do the work; this one confesses.
